hello my names vic fuentes

I am an American Musician, Singer, And songwriter
best known as the lead vocalist, rhythm guitarist, and primary songwriter of the band Pierce the veil.

Vic fuentes formend the band Pierce the veil with his brother, drummer mike fuentes in 2006 (after their previous band, Before today, broke up). known for his high-tenor vocal range and emotional, dramatic songwriting, vic led the band to massive commercial and critical success with recorda like A Flair for the Dramatic(2007), Collide with the Sky(2012),Selfish machines(2010),Misadventures(2016), and The jaws of life(2023).

About me

Vic fuentes was born on Febuary 10 1983 and raised in San Diego, California. His father, Victor Gamboa Fuentes Worked as a painter and former jazz musician. His mother, Vivian K Fuentes, is of Irish descent. He grew up in a big family, with his younger brother (and former bandmate) mike, two half-brothers, and a half-sister.

His father taught him how to lean the guitar at just Seven years old He heavily emphasized that you "Really feel the music" Which deeply shaped vic's emotional song writing style. Vic attends to Bay High school then later on went to San Diego state university where he studdied graphic design before he eventually drops out to start his early band.

In his youth he has struggled with depression and self-harm. He has shared about them in interviews that his major turning point came when he realized his actions negatively affected his younger brother. So he choose to stop and redirect all those thoughts into writing music.

Vic met his wife, model and actresse Danielle Victoria Perry, on the set of Pierce The Veil's iconic "king for a day" music video in 2012. The two started dating in 2014 after severak years, vic proposed and they legally got married on april 2 2022. They've started a family in san diego and welcomed their first child Violet Valentine on February 9 2023 ironically just a day before vics own birthday then they had a second child, a son named emery in febuary 2025
